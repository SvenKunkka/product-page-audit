# Generic product-page review checklist

Use only categories supported by the requested scope and available evidence. Record a finding when there is a demonstrated issue; otherwise record missing verification where material.

## Identity and variants

- Does the title, main heading and selected variant identify the same product?
- Are generation, regional layout, capacity, color or other meaningful suffixes preserved?
- Do source documents belong to that exact model and revision?
- Are linked manuals, downloads, comparison entries and recommendations relevant to the product?
- If SKU/EAN is in scope, does each identifier match the authoritative variant table?

## Specifications and claims

- Compare each model's important claims with its own current specification and configuration source.
- Look for conflicting numbers across feature copy, tables, images and variant data.
- Check units and conditions: dimensions, weight, power, capacity, compatibility, runtime and operating modes.
- Distinguish a source mismatch from an equivalent unit conversion or formatting difference.
- Treat marketing superlatives and performance guarantees as claims requiring evidence.
- Keep missing evidence explicit; do not fill product facts from memory or a neighboring model.

## Copy and media

- Check ambiguous wording, grammar, spelling and consistent naming.
- Identify stale product references that materially mislead a customer.
- Compare images with the described color, components, accessories and variant.
- Inspect text inside supplied images when resolution permits.
- Review useful alt text while allowing intentionally decorative images to have empty alt.
- Separate readability defects from optional editorial preferences.

## Navigation and purchase information

- Confirm that links refer to the intended product; an HTTP success code alone is insufficient.
- Compare displayed price, currency and availability with authoritative supplied records when available.
- Confirm that buyers can understand the selected variant and what is included.
- Check manuals, specifications, compatibility and support links within the requested scope.
- Do not infer private stock, shipping settings or checkout success from public markup.

## Desktop and mobile evidence

- Inspect desktop and mobile states actually captured, including viewport dimensions.
- Look for clipped text, unreadable tables, overlap and horizontal overflow.
- Check that important product information and purchase controls remain accessible.
- Assess overlays, menus or variant interaction only when their states have been observed.
- Reserve real-device conclusions for real-device evidence.

## Reporting

Prioritize defects by customer impact and the confidence of the evidence. Attach a location and a short fix to each finding. Keep failed or unperformed checks visible. A clean automatic report supports a review but cannot by itself approve a launch.
